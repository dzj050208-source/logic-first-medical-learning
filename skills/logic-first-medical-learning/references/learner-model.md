# Learner model

## Confirmed preferences

- Add only learning preferences the learner explicitly confirms or repeatedly demonstrates.
- Prefer the purpose of a method, its causal logic, and the distinction between surface procedure and scientific meaning.
- When the learner restates an idea, confirm the sound core and repair only the precise gap.
- Use a concrete example and a concise essence statement when they improve understanding.

## Validated reasoning patterns

Add short, generalizable patterns only after they have repeatedly helped the learner. Do not copy ordinary lesson content into this file.

## Maintenance rules

- Keep entries concise and transferable across medical subjects.
- Revise or remove an entry when the learner explicitly corrects it.
- Do not store grades, medical information, identity data, or other sensitive personal details.
